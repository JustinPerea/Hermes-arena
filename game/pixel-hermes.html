Hermes Arena · pixel style study

Pixel Hermes

Pixel-art fighters and the Altar of the Agora, all drawn entirely in code. Press attack on the title screen, then pick both fighters, the opponent (a CPU level, a second player, or a training dummy that stands still), the number of stocks and a time limit. Every sprite and the whole stage are built at load time from ellipses, capsules, feathers and polygons rasterised onto a pixel grid, then shaded with five-tone ramps and a 4 × 4 dither. No image files. Everything, from fighters to columns to the damage readout, sits on the same fine pixel grid. Spar on the altar, or study each animation frame by frame below.

AD or ←→ run Space jump; tap it (or press jump and attack together) for a short hop (Hermes has 2 jumps; Aethon has 3, and after his last one he can glide for up to 1 second by holding jump) Left click or J attack: no direction = jab (click again for jab 2 and jab 3; hold or keep mashing after jab 2 for a rapid jab), while running = dash attack, A/D = forward, W = up, S = down; in the air you get the five aerials (pressing away from where you face is the back air). Keep holding attack with a direction on the ground for a smash attack: the longer you hold (up to a second) the harder it hits Right click or K special: same four directions (side and up specials once per jump) Enter, P or Esc pause (Start on a controller); in menus the arrows choose, attack or Enter confirms, K or Esc goes back, and you can click M sound on or off (volume in Tuning) Controller (Xbox or PlayStation layout): left stick or d-pad move, A attack, B special, X/Y jump, LB/RB grab, LT/RT shield. Flick the right stick for a smash attack on the ground (hold it to charge) or an aerial in the air. Press a button once to wake the controller Two players: pick Player 2 as the opponent on the fighter screen. During the match the keyboard is split. Player 1: WASD move, F attack, G special, Space jump, left Shift shield, Q grab (and the mouse). Player 2: arrow keys move, J attack, K special, N jump, L or right Shift shield, U grab. One controller connected is player 2's; with two, the first is player 1's. Either player can pause S ↓ drop through a platform; in the air while falling, a fast fall Shift shield (it shrinks while held and breaks at zero). From shield: S spot dodge, A/D roll, Space jump, attack or Q grab. Shift in the air is an air dodge (once per jump; hold a direction to dash that way) Q or Shift + click grab; then attack to pummel, or a direction to throw (forward, back, up, down) Falling near the stage edge grabs the ledge: W or toward the stage climbs, Space jumps, attack climbs with a swing, Shift rolls in, S or away lets go Parry: let go of Shift just before a hit lands (within 5 frames) and it does nothing; the attacker freezes for a moment. A shrunken shield can be poked: hits that reach the body outside the bubble get through Hold S on the ground to crouch (hits launch 15% less), with A/D to crawl. Tapping a direction starts a quick dash; reverse during it to dash dance, or reverse and let go to pivot in place. Reversing out of a full run skids first Space on top of the foe's head is a footstool: you spring off without using a jump and knock them down. Launched into the side of the altar? Shift as you hit it to wall tech, or you bounce off Launched hard? Press Shift just before you land to tech (with A/D to tech roll). Miss it and you are knocked down: attack, roll or stand up

Design notes

Kept from the mascot

  • Steel helmet with feathered wings, eyes lost in the brim's shadow
  • Blue scarf over the lower face
  • Gold pauldrons, bracers and greaves
  • Feather cloak, blue outside and cream at the shoulders
  • Gold winged caduceus with a blue orb

Made our own

  • Three-quarter side view built for a fighter, not a front-facing portrait
  • Swept-back wings, a gold crest line and a single orb-blue eye glint
  • Scarf tail that streams behind him and reacts to motion
  • Talaria: little wings on the boots
  • Blue tabard with a gold edge, gold chevron sigil

Palette ramps

Aethon, the Winged Hoplite

  • Heavy all-rounder: the hardest fighter to kill, slow to start, long spear reach
  • Three jumps, and a glide when you hold jump while falling
  • Kit from the roster doc: spear (dory), round shield (aspis), feathered wings

Reading him at a glance

  • Bronze and crimson against Hermes's blue and gold, so the two never blur together
  • Corinthian helmet with a tall horsehair crest: the tallest silhouette on the roster
  • Big white wings that fold behind him and open wide in the air
  • Crimson shield with a white wing emblem held across his chest
  • Muscle cuirass, leather pteruges, bronze greaves, bare arms

Palette ramps

Kairos, Keeper of the Moment

  • Zoner and trapper: slow on foot and floaty, but controls space from far away
  • Hour Bolt charges and can be stored by shielding; Orrery Ring flies out and comes back
  • Stilled Instant: a circle where everything but Kairos moves at half speed
  • Stepped Moment: an eight-way warp recovery, untouchable while he is gone

Reading him at a glance

  • Violet robe and deep hood, amber eyes glowing in the shadow, white beard
  • Sand-coloured stole: his sand trails behind dash attack, down tilt and down air
  • Long wooden staff topped with a brass orrery that lights up when he charges

Palette ramps

Iris, the Rainbow Messenger

  • Fast rushdown: light and quick in the air, short reach, long combos
  • Prism Shards fan out three ways and stall her once per jump
  • Rainbow Arc bends up or down and cancels into an aerial
  • Bridge of Heaven leaves a rainbow ledge for 3 seconds; Styx Pour leaves a puddle that trips runners

Reading her at a glance

  • Pearl chiton with an aqua sash, so she reads light against the others
  • Rainbow wings and a ribbon of light that follows every swing
  • Bronze hydria pitcher in her other hand: the water moves
